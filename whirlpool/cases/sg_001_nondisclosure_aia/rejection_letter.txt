Dear Mr. Tan,

RE: VOIDING OF POLICY AND REJECTION OF CLAIM

We refer to your claim for laparoscopic appendicectomy for acute appendicitis.

During our assessment, records obtained from your general practitioner show that you were
seen in 2021 and 2022 for atopic eczema and prescribed a topical cream. This information
was not declared in your application dated 3 March 2023, where you answered "No" to the
question on any skin condition for which you had consulted a doctor.

As stated in the policy contract under "Voiding your policy", we will void the policy if
you failed to provide any information that would have affected our decision to provide
cover. Accordingly, your policy is void from the policy date and the claim is not payable.
Premiums paid, less any amount for MediShield Life, will be refunded.

If you disagree with our decision, you may write to us. You may also refer the matter to
the Financial Industry Disputes Resolution Centre (FIDReC).
