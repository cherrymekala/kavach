We have examined the claim documents submitted for Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy for
Cholelithiasis with Chronic Cholecystitis. As per the records, the insured was a known
case of gall stone disease prior to the commencement of the policy. Hence the claim is
not payable under Exclusion No.1 - Pre-Existing Diseases (Code Excl 01) of the policy,

continuous coverage.

Accordingly, the claim stands repudiated. If you are not satisfied, you may approach our
